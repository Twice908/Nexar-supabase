# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Employees commuting between home and their office, typically using Nexar for recurring daily rides.

## Product Purpose

Nexar helps employees coordinate shared rides for the home-to-office and office-to-home commute. Users need to understand their ride, the route hops, and their role in the car quickly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing product supports ride matching, driver and passenger roles, ride status, route hops, profiles, notifications, and recurring commute details. Preserve the Nexar name, existing behavior, and current visual theme. UI work may improve the user flow but must not change product logic.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Nexar name, logo assets, and incumbent visual theme.

## Product Principles

- Make the next commute action immediately clear.
- Keep driver and passenger responsibilities easy to scan.
- Preserve confidence through visible ride status and route details.
- Keep recurring commute setup straightforward.
